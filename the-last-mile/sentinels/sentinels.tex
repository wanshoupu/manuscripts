\chapter{Power of sentinels} \label{ch:sentinels}

\label{Evernote:Use of sentinel}
\label{Evernote:Missing Ranges}

Null terminated string is essentially a sentinel to tell us the end of string.
By doing so, the benefit is uniform treatment.

Many programming languages provide `getOrDefault' methods for their collection classes.
That's a disguised application of sentinel.

\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
    \lstinputlisting[label={lst:getOrDefault},caption={An example of sentinel as a programming
    language feature.}]{sentinels/getOrDefault.java}
\end{minipage}

Sentinels are often used to cure the so-called `loop-and-a-half' problem.
In the part ~\fullref{prt:advanced}, particularly ~\autoref{ch:json-prettifier}, application of
sentinel is used extensively.
