\chapter{View \ilcode{for} and \ilcode{while} loops as a carousel} \label{ch:loops}

Loops are the powerhouse, the amplifier of computer works.
Using loop can boost productivity $n$-times by coding up the loop-block once and execute it
$n$-times.
The relevant parts of a loop of generic construct are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Pre-loop section
    \item Termination condition
    \item Loop body
    \item \code{break} logic \& \code{continue} logic
    \item Post-loop section
\end{itemize}
Depending on the situation, a loop may or may not have certain parts as mentioned above.
